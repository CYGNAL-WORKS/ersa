# Workspaces: lessons for later

- **Reclaiming abandoned workspaces.** Settled in FLOW-CLEAN-UP and ADR 0038: Core sweeps, and removes unasked only what holds no unsaved work. What shaped it, a sweep that has worked: run on a standing interval after every session has declared its hold, log why each worktree is kept, and keep anything uncertain. Reclaim a worktree whose work is pushed, merged, or held by another ref, whose upstream is gone, that has no commits of its own, or that sat idle past a grace period. Sort outside the lock and remove under it; sorting fifty under the lock stalled worktree creation past timeouts.
- **Removing one.** Every removal is now one routine (PROTO-CORE-API, Removing a folder). Still to say: what happens to other processes rooted in the folder, such as terminal-pane shells: stop every process whose working directory or executable is inside it, except Ersa itself.
- **Default base.** Settled in PROTO-CORE-API (Workspaces): without a `base`, `workspace.create` starts from the default branch (the local branch `origin/HEAD` points at, else `main`, else `master`), else from the checked-out commit.
