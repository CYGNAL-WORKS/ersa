# The workbench and the developer pane

How the workbench and its first pane look, token by token. `LAY-SHELL` says where the workbench sits and what opens it, `FLOW-READ-RAW-LOG` how the developer pane behaves, and `DESIGN.md` holds the tokens. Read this before changing either.

No board on the design canvas draws these, and Claude Desktop has no developer pane, so this page is the reference: where the built components or the gallery differ from it, this page wins. The owner reviews the pane in the gallery, in both themes, once it is built.

## The workbench

- **The region** fills the window's height at its right edge, on `surface`, with a `line` hairline at its leading edge and no radius: panes meet at lines. It is `size-workbench` wide when first opened. Its limits (FLOW-READ-RAW-LOG-R16) are `size-workbench-max` of the width it shares with the transcript area and, while that allows it, `size-workbench-min`.
- **Opening and closing.** Its width grows from nothing over `motion-settle` with `motion-ease-out` while the transcript area narrows over the same, and closing runs it back. With reduced motion on it appears and leaves at once.
- **The resize edge** is the hairline itself. A strip `space-sm` wide, centered on the line, takes the pointer and shows the column-resize cursor. Under the pointer the line turns `ink-faint`; while dragged, and with keyboard focus, `ink`. Dragging follows the pointer with no easing. It is named "Resize workbench"; Left and Right move it by `space-lg`, Home and End to its least and greatest width.
- **The head** is a row `size-control` high, padded `space-sm` by `space-md`, with a `line` hairline beneath: the pane's name in `ui` at the `heading` weight in `ink`, and at the trailing edge Close, an icon button `size-control` square with an x icon at `size-icon-sm`, its tooltip and accessible name "Close". An icon button is a control without an edge (`DESIGN.md`, Pointer and press).
- **Keyboard focus** never falls to the window when a control takes itself away: closing the workbench from inside it sends focus to the arrange button; closing the detail, by Close line or Escape, sends it to the list; and so does choosing Jump to latest (FLOW-READ-RAW-LOG-R20).
- **In the arrange menu**, "Developer pane" sits above Settings in the last group and shows the check of an item that is on (`RAIL.md`, Menus) while the workbench is open.

## The developer pane

The pane's name is "Developer". Beneath the head, from top to bottom: the toolbar, the list, and the detail while a line is selected.

- **The toolbar** is padded `space-sm` by `space-md`, its parts `space-sm` apart: the filter field; the count after it, while the field holds text; then Open diagnostic log at the trailing edge. The field and the count keep together and share the width the button leaves, the field taking what the count leaves, so the count grows in out of the field. The toolbar is one row while it can give those two twice `size-row-title-min`, the field's least width and as much again for the longest count, beside the button. Narrower, as it is at the workbench's least width, Open diagnostic log moves to a second row `space-sm` beneath, at its trailing edge, and the field and the count have the first to themselves. Which it is follows the pane's width alone, never what is typed, so the list doesn't move while the user types. Neither the count nor the button is ever cut: the field is the part that gives way, down to its icon, and what is typed scrolls inside it.
- **The filter field** is the sessions page's search field (`RAIL.md`), with a filter icon at `size-icon-sm` in `ink-faint` before the words. Its placeholder and accessible name are "Filter lines". Escape empties it.
- **The count** reads "{shown} of {total}" in `ui-small`, `ink-muted` and tabular figures.
- **Open diagnostic log** is a quiet button: its label in `ui-small` and `ink-muted`, `size-control` high, `radius-md`, padded `space-sm`, with no edge. Under the pointer it takes `material-hover` and reads `ink`, pressed `material-pressed`, and with keyboard focus its edge turns `ink`. Unavailable, it reads `ink-faint`, doesn't change under the pointer, and its tooltip gives the reason.
- **A problem** is one line that lies over the top of the list, on `surface-raised` with a `line` hairline beneath, so no row moves when it comes or goes. It is padded `space-xs` by `space-md`: a dot at `size-glyph` in `danger`, the message in `ui-small` and `ink`, then the reason in `ink-faint`, cut with an ellipsis and whole in its tooltip. It fades in and out over `motion-quick` with `motion-ease-out`. The pane has two. That the raw log can't be read stays while reads fail (FLOW-READ-RAW-LOG-R15). That the diagnostic log couldn't be opened stays until the user's next press or key in the toolbar, the list or the detail, a change of active session, or the user closing the workbench (FLOW-READ-RAW-LOG-R26). While both stand they are two lines, the read's first and the open's beneath it (FLOW-READ-RAW-LOG-R25): neither hides the other, and the first never moves. Each comes and goes by itself, and assistive technology hears each once, when it comes.

### The list

- **A row** is `size-log-row` high, padded `space-md` at the sides, its parts `space-sm` apart, edge to edge with no radius and no rule between rows. From the leading edge: the time as 24-hour `HH:MM:SS.mmm` in `ui-small`, `ink-faint` and tabular figures; the direction mark; the line's start in `mono` and `ink`, on one line, cut with an ellipsis at its end. A row draws at most the line's first 2,000 characters, counted as the platform counts a string's length, in UTF-16 code units. A character that takes two units is never cut in half; one the reader sees as a single character but that is made of several, such as an emoji joined from others or a letter with a combining mark, may be.
- **The direction mark** is an arrow at `size-icon-xs` in `ink-muted`: pointing down and left for a line from the backend, up and right for a line to it. Shape carries the meaning, not color. Assistive technology hears "From {backend}" or "To {backend}", with the backend's own label.
- **Under the pointer** a row takes `material-hover`. **Selected**, it takes `material-open-session`, the rail's quiet highlight for the open session, the same with keyboard focus as without. A selected line the list doesn't show, left out by the filter or no longer held, has no row to highlight, and its detail stays (FLOW-READ-RAW-LOG-R21).
- **With keyboard focus** the list has an `ink` hairline edge, drawn inside its area, as a control without an edge does (`DESIGN.md`, Pointer and press), whether or not a row is selected. The mark is the list's and never a row's: the selected row can be out of view, scrolled away from or left out by the filter, and the list's edge can't.
- **Earlier lines are gone**: the first row reads "Earlier lines are no longer held." in `ui-small` and `ink-faint`, centered, and can't be selected.
- **Jump to latest** floats over the list's foot, centered, `space-md` above it: a pill `size-control` high on `surface-raised` with a `line` edge and `radius-pill`, padded `space-md` at the sides, holding an arrow-down icon at `size-icon-sm`, "Jump to latest" in `ui-small` and `ink`, and, once a line has arrived since the user scrolled away, the count of new lines after it in `ink-muted` and tabular figures ("3 new"). It never reads "0 new". Its accessible name starts with the words it shows: "Jump to latest", then "Jump to latest, 3 new lines". It casts no shadow: it is a control inside its pane, not a transient layer that opens over the page, so it takes no `stack` token either (`DESIGN.md`, Elevation & Depth). It is a control with an edge: under the pointer its edge turns `ink-faint` and it takes `material-tile`, pressed it sinks to `canvas` and its edge turns `ink-muted`, and with keyboard focus its edge turns `ink`. It fades in and out over `motion-quick` with `motion-ease-out`.
- **New rows** appear at once: a row that faded in would blur a fast log, and the list is not prose. Returning to the end, by Jump to latest or End, is at once too.
- **Nothing to list** is said in the middle of the list's area, in `ui` and `ink-muted`, centered, at most `size-dialog` wide. With no active session: "No session is open. Choose a session to see its raw log." With no lines: "No lines yet. Lines appear here while the session runs." Before the first answer: "Reading…" With a filter no line contains: "No lines contain that text."

### The detail

- **The detail** takes the lower half of the pane beneath the toolbar, divided from the list by a `line` hairline, and grows in over `motion-settle` with `motion-ease-out`; as it grows in, the list brings the selected row into view. It stays while its line is selected, whether or not the list shows that line, and says nothing more then: its head still gives the line's direction, time and size.
- **Its head** is a row `size-control` high, padded `space-xs` by `space-md`: "From {backend}" or "To {backend}", the time, and the line's size as `CONTENT.md` writes a number with its unit (1.2 KB, 12 MB), in `ui-small` and `ink-muted`, `space-sm` apart. At the trailing edge are Copy, the transcript's Copy (`TRANSCRIPT.md`) with the tooltip and accessible name "Copy line", then an icon button with an x icon named "Close line", which clears the selection.
- **The line** is `mono` in `ink`, padded `space-md`, its lines kept and wrapping wherever they must, so the detail scrolls down and never sideways. The scrolling text takes keyboard focus, so it scrolls from the keyboard, and then has an `ink` hairline edge drawn inside it, as the list does.
- **A cut line** says above its text, in `ui-small` and `ink-muted`: "This line is {size}. Its first 256 KB are shown."
